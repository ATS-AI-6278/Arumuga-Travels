## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, dependable, and warm digital presence for an outstation taxi and travel desk based in temple-town Srivilliputtur, Tamil Nadu. The visual identity bridges deep-rooted local hospitality with the crisp efficiency of modern travel coordination. It moves deliberately away from the cold, impersonal aesthetic of urban rideshare apps, leaning instead into reassuring human connection, transparent rates, and bespoke long-distance road journey planning.

The design movement is **Warm Editorial & Tactile Modern**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, honorable, warm, and hyper-reliable.
- **Atmosphere:** Earthy temple hues, sunlit highway horizons, and tactile paper-like cream containers that evoke traditional route ledgers combined with contemporary mobile convenience.
- **Key Tenets:** 
  1. *Immediate Accessibility:* Instantaneous Call and WhatsApp triggers with prominent contact channels.
  2. *Tactile Reassurance:* Physical card layers with gentle borders, subtle tonal warmth, and clean vehicle specifications (passenger count, luggage volume, AC confirmation).
  3. *Typographic Heritage:* Classical high-legibility serif headings paired with clean, utilitarian humanist sans-serif body typography.

## Colors

The color system is derived from Tamil Nadu’s architectural warmth, temple terracotta, and highway sunbeams:

- **Primary (`#C86337` - Amber Terracotta):** Used for decisive action states, primary buttons, key route accents, and badge highlights. Communicates grounded authority and human warmth.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26` - Temple Gold):** Used for auxiliary highlights, rating indicators, heritage tags, and subtle metallic borders on premium vehicle cards.
- **Dark Neutral (`#1F1A17` - Deep Charcoal):** The bedrock for typography, dark hero backdrops, footers, and inverted callout panels. Provides contrast and dignity.
- **Light Neutral (`#F6F1E8` - Warm Ivory / Ledger Cream):** The foundation canvas. It eliminates stark clinical white in favor of a warm, welcoming surface.
- **Functional Touches:** Dedicated WhatsApp green (`#25D366`) is preserved strictly for direct messaging actions to guarantee global familiarity and trust.

### Contrast & States
- Primary interactive elements use white text (`#FFFFFF`) against `#C86337` to exceed WCAG AAA standards.
- Secondary cards use `#FCFAF6` resting over `#F6F1E8` framed with subtle `#E3DACB` borders to sustain tactile structure without harsh drop shadows.

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of **Newsreader** with the workhorse legibility of **Source Sans 3**, accented by **JetBrains Mono** for travel dispatches, vehicle numbers, and tariff parameters.

- **Newsreader (Headlines & Section Titles):** Imparts generational trust and narrative warmth. Headings feel crafted, thoughtful, and steady—reassuring families planning outstation pilgrimages or airport transfers.
- **Source Sans 3 (Body Text & Form Inputs):** Clear, approachable, and balanced. High legibility across all screen densities under bright outdoor lighting conditions.
- **JetBrains Mono (Metadata & Dispatches):** Used sparingly for registration codes, small pill tags (e.g., `TN 84 F 6278`, `4 SEATER + AC`), pickup counters, and time stamps, evoking authentic journey waybills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a balanced fluid 12-column grid on desktop (max width: 1200px) that simplifies into a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Rhythm & Padding:** Spacing follows an intentional 4px/8px modular rhythm. Cards and hero banners employ generous inner breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish an unhurried, reassuring presence.
- **Mobile First Adaptation:** Outer margins adjust from `1.5rem` (mobile) to `2.5rem` (desktop). Floating action dock bars anchor the bottom safe area on mobile devices to preserve immediate contact with the travel desk without obstructing content.
- **Section Breaks:** Sections are partitioned with soft horizontal borders (`#E3DACB`) and ambient warm backdrops rather than sharp visual cutoffs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **tonal paper surfaces and delicate hairline boundaries**, avoiding heavy generic drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background `#F6F1E8`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Tactile cards and inquiry pods sit on `#FCFAF6` with a 1px border of `#E3DACB`. A featherweight ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.04)`) separates card elements.
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Targets):** Mobile CTA docks and dropdown menus utilize frosted background containment with high-density warm tinting (`rgba(252, 250, 246, 0.94)`, backdrop blur 12px) paired with an edge outline (`#E3DACB`) and soft shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.08)`).
- **Hero / Dark Elevation:** Dark contrast panels use rich charcoal `#1F1A17` with hairline terracotta inner borders (`rgba(200, 99, 55, 0.2)`), imparting a prestigious twilight look.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** corner radius language. 

- **Containers & Fleet Cards:** 0.375rem (6px) to 0.5rem (8px), projecting structured craftsmanship and alignment with physical ticket envelopes and destination logbooks.
- **Buttons & Chips:** Pill elements (such as vehicle tags or call badges) are refined with 9999px fully rounded radii for touch-friendly ergonomics. Form inputs and card modules remain structured with 6px corner radiuses.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#C86337`, label `#FFFFFF`, hover `#A64B23`. Generous padding (`0.75rem 1.5rem`), font `cta-button`, subtly rounded (6px).
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Background `#25D366` or pill container `#FCFAF6` with WhatsApp green icon and border. Instant trigger for messaging.
- **Call Desk Button:** Deep charcoal `#1F1A17` with white type and warm terracotta telephone glyph.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined with 1px `#C86337`, text `#C86337`, transparent fill.

### Fleet & Destination Cards
- **Vehicle Showcase:** `#FCFAF6` container, 1px `#E3DACB` outline. High-resolution vehicle side/front profile image on top. Metadata badge row using `label-caps` in pill containers: passenger capacity, luggage limit, fuel/AC status, and vehicle registration mono tag.
- **Destination Route Strip:** Horizontal interactive chip list for popular hubs (Madurai, Chennai, Rameswaram, Kanyakumari, Tirunelveli, Courrallam). Clickable to instantly prime the route inquiry form.

### Inquiry & Booking Inputs
- **Form Fields:** Neutral `#FFFFFF` fill framed in 1px `#E3DACB`, focused state shifts border to `#C86337` with 2px soft ring. Labels are displayed in crisp `body-sm` Charcoal Muted. Clear placeholder text indicates sample pickups (e.g., *Srivilliputtur Bus Stand*, *Madurai Airport*).

### Service Feature Tiles
- Minimalist ivory cards with inset terracotta icons (sedan, luggage, temple gopuram, clock), paired with a bold `headline-sm` title and short narrative body explaining outstation pacing, temple itineraries, or airport guarantee.

### Mobile Quick-Access Floating Dock
- Persistent bottom bar anchoring two balanced touchpoints: **Call Desk** and **WhatsApp Us**, ensuring travellers on slow network conditions or urgent travel requests can reach a live operator with one tap.
